## The Idea

A pothole gets ignored.

A streetlight stops working.

A drain gets blocked.

People report it, wait for the authorities, and often have no idea **what happens next**.

### Nirmaan imagines a different system.

A platform where citizens can **report a civic problem**, understand its severity, discover what it may take to fix, and connect it with people or organizations capable of solving it.

## What is Nirmaan?

**Nirmaan** is a civic-tech platform designed around one simple idea:

> **Don't just report the problem. Help move it toward a solution.**

The platform creates a bridge between **citizens, engineers, NGOs, contractors, and companies** so that local infrastructure problems can move from a complaint to an actionable solution.

---

## The Problem

Local civic issues are everywhere, but solving them can be difficult because of:

- Problems going unreported
- Lack of visibility after reporting
- Unclear severity and urgency
- Limited access to people who can fix them
- Funding constraints
- Difficulty coordinating communities and organizations

A citizen may know **what is wrong**, but not necessarily **who can fix it, how much it might cost, or how to make it happen**.

That's where Nirmaan comes in.

## The Nirmaan Ecosystem

Nirmaan is designed around multiple roles, each contributing to the same goal.

| Role | What They Do |
|:---|:---|
| **Citizen** | Report problems, contribute funds, earn rewards |
| **Engineer** | Discover and accept repair opportunities |
| **NGO** | Handle community-scale civic problems |
| **Company** | Contribute through CSR funding |
| **Platform** | Analyze, match, coordinate and track |

The idea is to turn individual reports into **community-driven action**.

---

## Core Features

### Report Civic Problems

Citizens can report issues from their local area by providing:

- Problem photograph
- Location
- Relevant details

Examples include:

```text
Potholes
Dead Streetlights
Blocked Drains
Damaged Roads
Other Civic Infrastructure Issues
```

---

### AI-Powered Analysis

The long-term vision is to use AI/ML to automatically analyze reported problems.

```text
Image
  ↓
Computer Vision
  ↓
Problem Classification
  ↓
Severity Detection
  ↓
Estimated Repair Cost
```

This can help prioritize problems based on their urgency and potential impact.

---

### Smart Matching

Once a problem is analyzed, Nirmaan aims to connect it with the most relevant people or organizations.

```text
Civic Problem
      ↓
Location + Category + Severity
      ↓
Matching System
      ↓
Engineer / NGO / Contractor / Company
```

---

### Community Funding

Some problems may require resources to be solved.

Nirmaan's vision includes community-driven funding where people and organizations can contribute toward solving verified civic problems.

```text
Problem Report
      ↓
Estimated Cost
      ↓
Funding Goal
      ↓
Community Contributions
      ↓
Repair
```

Companies could also contribute through **CSR funding**.

---

### Rewards & Participation

To encourage continued participation, users can earn **points and rewards** for meaningful contributions.

```text
Report
  ↓
Contribute
  ↓
Help Solve
  ↓
Earn Points
  ↓
Stay Engaged
  ↓
Improve Your Community
```

## Future AI / ML

The AI/ML layer is planned as one of the major components of Nirmaan.

| AI / ML Module | Purpose |
|:---|:---|

| Severity Model | Estimate urgency / severity |

These components will be developed and integrated as the project evolves.
